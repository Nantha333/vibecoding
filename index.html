Production disciplines

Six disciplines that turn a prototype into a platform.

Each one closes a gap that AI builders leave open. Your health check sets the priority, so the highest business risk is resolved first.

01

Data Governance & Access Control

Every user, tenant and role sees exactly what they should, and nothing more.

  • Tenant isolation and row-level security
  • Server-enforced authentication and authorization
  • Continuous vulnerability scanning
02

Secrets & Credential Management

Credentials leave the client and move into a governed vault.

  • Secrets centralized server-side
  • Automated rotation with full audit trails
  • Least-privilege service identities
03

Quality Engineering & Release Assurance

Change at AI speed, without regressions ever reaching customers.

  • Automated coverage of revenue-critical journeys
  • CI/CD with mandatory review gates
  • Staging parity and instant rollback
04

Payments & Integration Integrity

Every transaction, refund and sync reconciles, every time.

  • Idempotent payment and retry handling
  • Verified, replay-safe webhooks
  • Reconciliation against your payment provider
05

AI Governance & Cost Control

Model-powered features that stay accurate, safe and financially predictable.

  • Usage budgets with real-time alerts
  • Input and output guardrails
  • Model fallback and graceful degradation
06

Scalability, Observability & Resilience

An architecture that holds under launch-day load and recovers by design.

  • Cloud infrastructure sized for production traffic
  • Monitoring, tracing and on-call alerting
  • Tested backup and disaster-recovery runbooks

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still wondering if your app is ready? A health check answers that fast.

My app already works. Why do I need this?

Working for you is not the same as working for thousands of users with real data and real money. The health check shows exactly what could break, and what to fix first.

Will you throw away what I built?

Rarely. Most AI-built apps can be strengthened rather than rewritten. If a rebuild is the smarter path, we explain why before anything starts.

Can I keep prompting while you work?

Yes. With “Build side by side” you keep shipping with your AI tool, and we turn each version into a tested, launch-ready release.

It was built by a freelancer, not AI. Can you still help?

Yes. We work with code from AI builders, freelancers and previous vendors alike.

Do you cover compliance like HIPAA, GDPR or DPDP?

We can prepare your app for these requirements, and Mako is ISO 27001 certified. Please confirm your exact obligations with your legal team.

What happens after launch?

We can stay on to monitor, support and keep shipping features, or hand everything to your own team with full documentation.

How do we work out the cost?

After the health check, you get a scoped plan and cost before any hardening begins.
